## Core Features
- **Substance & Pharmacokinetic Telemetry:** Log caffeine, alcohol, and all 5 tobacco modalities (cigarettes, cigars with common/custom vitola sizes, chew, pipes, and vapes) with drink start/end times and `mL` / `fl oz` conversion.
- **Google Health / Fitbit Food & Barcode Database:** Search caffeine and alcohol beverages by UPC/EAN barcode or text entry.
- **Combined Safe Sleep & Sleep Readiness Score Graph:** 24-hour clearance timeline with interactive layer toggles (`Sleep Readiness Score`, `Caffeine`, `Alcohol`, `Tobacco`).
- **AI Sleep Coach & Circadian Sleep Reminders:** Server-side Gemini AI sleep recommendations and target-bedtime calibrated cutoff reminders.
- **Wearable Sync, Biometric Lock & Google Drive Encrypted Vault:** Apple HealthKit & Google Fit sleep stage correlation, WebAuthn passkey lock, and AES-GCM-256 encrypted backups to Google Drive.
